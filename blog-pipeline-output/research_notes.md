## Mumbai-Specific Data Points

### Pricing (2026)
- BWR Plywood: ₹1,800–₹2,500 per sq ft
- BWP (Boiling Water Proof) Plywood: ₹1,500–₹2,500 per sq ft
- Waterproof paint (Asian Paints Apex Ultima): ₹280–₹340 per litre
- Basic modular kitchen (Mumbai 2026): ₹1.2 lakh starting
- Mid-range modular kitchen: ₹1.8–₹4 lakh
- Interior designer consultation (Mumbai): ₹300–₹1,500 per sq ft
- Local carpenter with BWP plywood + Hettich hardware: ₹12,000–₹18,000 per running foot
- Branded showroom equivalent: ₹18,000–₹28,000 per running foot

### Mumbai Challenges
- Coastal humidity year-round
- 4-month monsoon (June–September)
- Neighboring towers blocking ventilation in high-rises
- Salt-laden coastal air corroding fixtures
- Compact apartment sizes requiring smart storage
- MDF swelling within 2 monsoon cycles
- Musty odours from trapped moisture
