## Core Services

### 1. Interior Design (Turnkey)
Complete home/office design + execution: space planning, 3D renders, material selection, civil, carpentry, electrical, painting. One team from concept to handover.
- **Typical cost:** ₹3.5L (1BHK) to ₹12L+ (3BHK)
- **Timeline:** 30-90 days
- **Warranty:** 10-year structural, 5-year waterproofing, 2-year electrical/painting

### 2. Modular Kitchen
Factory-made cabinets (L/U/parallel/straight layouts) with BWP plywood, laminate/acrylic/veneer finishes, Hettich/Hafele hardware, granite/quartz counters.
- **Cost:** ₹1.5L - ₹4L
- **Timeline:** 3-5 weeks

### 3. Custom Furniture
Made-to-measure wardrobes, beds, sofa-cum-storage, study tables, TV units using BWP plywood, laminate/veneer/PU finishes, branded hardware.
- **Cost:** ₹50k - ₹3L per piece/set
- **Timeline:** 2-4 weeks

### 4. Professional Painting
Interior/exterior walls, textures, waterproofing, colour consultation. Asian Paints/Berger/Nerolac premium emulsions.
- **Cost:** ₹15-40/sq ft (₹25k-1.5L for 2BHK)
- **Timeline:** 5-10 days

### 5. Electrical Works
New wiring, rewiring, lighting design, switchboards, inverter/fan/AC points, MCB/ELCB safety. Polycab/Finolex wires, Legrand/Schneider switches.
- **Cost:** ₹30k - ₹1.5L
- **Timeline:** 5-12 days

### 6. Floor Plan Design
2D CAD layouts, furniture placement, circulation plans for society approvals and contractor quotes.
- **Cost:** ₹5k - ₹25k
- **Timeline:** 3-7 days

### 7. Space Saving Solutions
Wall beds, lofts, foldable tables, floor-to-ceiling wardrobes for compact 1BHK/2BHK Mumbai apartments.
- **Cost:** ₹50k - ₹2L
- **Timeline:** 2-4 weeks
