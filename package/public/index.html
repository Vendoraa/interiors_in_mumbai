Interiors in Mumbai - Interior Design and Turnkey Contractors

Shrishti Interiors (Interiors in Mumbai) is an interior design and turnkey contracting firm based in Mira Road East. We started in 2009 and have completed more than 1,200 homes across Mumbai and Mira-Bhayandar.

Our Services

  • Full Home Interiors: Space planning, 3D designs, carpentry, false ceiling, electrical, plumbing and painting for flats in Mumbai.
  • Modular Kitchens: L-shape, U-shape, parallel and straight kitchens with BWP plywood, soft-close hardware and granite or quartz counters.
  • Custom Furniture: Wardrobes, beds, TV units and study tables built to your wall sizes.
  • Painting, Electrical and Civil Work: Repainting, rewiring, tiling and waterproofing, handled by our own in-house teams.

Service Areas

We work across Mumbai and the suburbs, including Mira Road, Bhayandar, Borivali, Andheri, Bandra, Powai, Thane and Navi Mumbai.
