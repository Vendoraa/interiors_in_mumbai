FAQ

Get Every Answer From Here

What is your process for working with a client?

We start with a free site visit to measure your flat and understand what you need. Then we share layout options, 3D views and a fixed quote. Once you approve, we order materials and start work. The five stages are: 1. Site visit, 2. Layout and 3D design, 3. Material selection, 4. Execution, 5. Final handover.

How do you ensure the quality of your work?

We use branded materials only: Century or Greenply plywood, Merino or Royal Touch laminates, Hettich or Hafele hardware, and Asian Paints Royale. Our carpenters are in-house, not outsourced, and a supervisor checks the site against a 50-point list before we hand over.

Can you customize your services to fit my specific needs?

Yes. We do both turnkey projects and design-only packages, so you can pick what suits your budget and timeline. Some clients only want a modular kitchen or a wardrobe; others want the whole flat done.

Do you offer any warranties on your work?

Yes, and it is written into the contract. Civil and carpentry work carries a 10-year warranty, waterproofing 5 years, and painting and electrical 2 years. Brand warranties on hardware and fittings are passed on to you along with the bills.

What are your payment terms?

We offer flexible payment options: 10% booking amount, 40% at material delivery, 40% during work progress, and 10% upon completion. We accept bank transfers, cheques, and UPI. We also assist with EMI options through banking partners.

Do you offer a lowest price guarantee?

Yes. If you get a lower written quote from another firm for the same scope and materials, we will match it. The quote has to be for identical specifications, so compare the plywood grade and hardware brands before you do.
